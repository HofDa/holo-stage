Holo Stage

Tell a story in the pyramid

A story is a row of scenes. Animations play by themselves. In between, the story stops and waits for the group: everyone holds their button together, agrees on a quiz answer, or takes turns.

Or add your videos under Videos and build your own below.

    Players

    One player per pyramid face. Give each the color of their physical button: the hologram uses it to show who is who.

    Video library

    Drop video files anywhere on this panel. Videos are stored in this browser, so they keep working offline. Use the plain, upright video on a black background. The app rotates and flips it for each face.
